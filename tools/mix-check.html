Mix check

Renders songs from every floor offline at the Volume slider's setting and measures the output: sample peak, plain RMS and BS.1770 loudness (LUFS, from M.loudness), skipping the first 5 seconds for RMS and loudness. "pre" columns are measured before the output limiter; maxGR is the most it took off a 400 ms block (dB) and lim% the share of blocks where it took over 0.5 dB. The trim column suggests a new FLOOR_MIX trim that brings each floor to the target. Serve the repository (npm run serve) and open /tools/mix-check.html.
