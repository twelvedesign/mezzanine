# Song Form Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Give Mezzanine songs a long-range shape: endless songs that move through Intro → Theme → Variation → Bridge → Breather → Return, then start a new round, with a melody that reaches every chord of the loop.

**Architecture:** The form is pure, seeded planning in `js/evolve.js` (`M.Evolve.planChapter`, `sectionBars`, `lineup`) walked by the `Conductor`, which tells the engine the current section through a new `setSection(sec)` call. The engine plays the Bridge's own progression (`song.progB`) during bridge sections and counts steps from the section start, so `M.partStep` keeps deciding every note. Auto-arrange turns the form on; off plays the plain loop as written.

**Tech Stack:** Plain browser JavaScript (classic scripts sharing `window.M`), Web Audio, `node --test`.

**Spec:** The design decisions stated in chat on 2026-10-23 (the user skipped the design conversation), summarised under Design below. Review findings that motivate it: the melody played over 51% of chords and over the last chord in 14 of 480 songs; Auto-arrange re-rolled the line-up every 8 bars; Evolve swapped progression or key at random 32-bar lines; the chord display lagged Evolve's changes.

## Design

- **Sections** (kind → harmony, energy, melody): intro (A, 1, off), theme (A, 3, on), variation (A, 3, on), bridge (B, 2 or 3, on half the time), breather (A, 1, on 35% of the time), return (A, 3, on). Round 0 starts with the intro; later rounds start at the theme and may skip the variation (30%) or the breather (25%). Labels: Intro, Theme, Variation, Bridge, Breather, Return.
- **Lengths:** each floor has a `pace` in bars (night, lobby, cafe, rain 16; tea, roof 24; focus, cloud 32). Theme, variation, bridge and return last `pace` rounded up to whole loops of their progression; the breather half that (at most `pace`); the intro 4 bars rounded up to a loop, at most 8.
- **Line-ups:** energy 1 = pad, texture, arp; 2 adds bass and keys; 3 adds drums. Each part beyond pad and texture sits out a section 15% of the time. The melody follows the section's own rule.
- **Bridge harmony:** `song.progB`, 2–4 chords (`min(4, max(2, prog.length))`), first chord away from the theme's first chord (IV, vi, ii, iii weighted 4/3/2/1), last chord leading back to it (a move with `NEXT[last][home] >= 2`), no diminished chords. Shown and editable as a second chord row.
- **Evolve with the form** acts only where sections start, seeded from song seed + floor + round + section: a new round's theme may bring a new melody, new chords (theme and bridge) or a key change; the variation varies the melody; the bridge may rewrite the arp or keys; the return brings the theme's melody back. Inside long sections, every 16 bars, a 35% chance of one new arp, keys, bass or drums pattern. With Auto-arrange off, Evolve keeps its old every-8-bars behaviour.
- **Melody breathing:** two bars on, two off; when the loop is a multiple of 4 bars, every other pass of the loop swaps the halves, so the answer phrase also lands on the last chord.
- **Drum fills:** on the last bar of every 8 within a section, and on a section's last bar.

## Global Constraints

- No dependencies, no bundler; classic scripts sharing `window.M`; the page keeps working from `file://`.
- `js/core.js` and `js/export.js` stay pure (no DOM, no Web Audio); `js/evolve.js` needs only an engine object with `on`, `rebuild`, `setArrangement` and (new) `setSection`.
- `M.partStep` decides every note for both `Engine.schedule` and `M.songNotes`; the "MIDI plays the same notes as the engine" test keeps passing.
- Song codes are a public format: add `pb` (bridge, same `d:e,d:e` format as `p`); codes without it decode to a bridge generated from the seed and the decoded theme, the same one every time.
- Unedited songs keep every existing field identical (the bridge draws from its own `seed:floor:bridge` random stream).
- `npm test` passes; offline renders peak below 1.0 with no NaN.

## Review Focus

- Editing chords (add, remove, change bars per chord) while a section plays: the next section's length follows the new loop; nothing jumps an octave or throws.
- Turning Auto-arrange off mid-bridge returns to the theme's loop; turning it on mid-loop joins at the theme on the next loop start.
- A new song chosen while playing (switch at the bar) restarts from the intro, never mid-bridge of the old song.
- Old shelf codes without `pb` load, and load to the same bridge every time.
- A part muted by the user stays silent whatever the line-up says.
